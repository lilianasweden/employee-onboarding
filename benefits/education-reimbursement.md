# Education Reimbursement — Quick Summary

The company reimburses eligible courses, certifications, and degree programmes.

## Key Facts
- Up to **€5,000/year** for role-critical courses (100% reimbursed)
- **AI, Machine Learning, and Data Engineering** courses are explicitly eligible as of 2026
- Pre-approval required **before** enrolling
- Submit claim within 90 days of completion

## Full Policy
The full policy including process steps, required documents, and the clawback policy is published on the HR Knowledge Base intranet.

## How to Apply
1. Submit a pre-approval request in **Workday** (People & Culture → Learning → Education Reimbursement)
2. Enrol and complete the course
3. Submit your claim in Workday with receipt and certificate
4. Reimbursement paid with your next payroll
